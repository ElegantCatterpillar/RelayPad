# What's next for RelayPad

**English** · [Español (México)](es/ROADMAP.md)

RelayPad's goal is simple: every Nintendo Switch controller, working on PC with all of its
features. Here is what is coming. Priorities can change as we test on real hardware, and your
[ideas](https://github.com/ElegantCatterpillar/RelayPad/issues) help decide the order.

Already out: the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md). Working today: the
[controller table](README.md#controllers).

## App

### Next

- **A smoother install:** a signed installer, so Windows stops warning about it.
- **Clearer status:** tray icon per controller, and tips for the best Bluetooth connection.
- **Low-battery notice** before your controller dies mid-game.

### Later

- **More ways to show up in games:** DirectInput and GameInput next to Xbox 360 and DualShock 4.
- **3D controller view** in the app.
- **Beyond Windows:** Linux, macOS and Android are being explored.
