Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

RelayPad's YouTube videos are uploaded with yt-upload, a private script used only by the channel owner. This policy covers that script's use of Google user data.

Looking for the RelayPad app? It needs no account and works offline. See Privacy in the user guide.

What it accesses

yt-upload requests one Google permission: "Manage your YouTube videos" (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload). It does not request your name, email address, contacts or any other Google account data, and it never accesses other people's accounts.

How it uses the data

The permission is used only to upload videos to the owner's own channel and set their thumbnails. Nothing it receives from Google is used for advertising, analytics, profiling or training AI models.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. It is never sent to any server other than Google's, and it is never shared, sold or transferred to anyone.

Retention and deletion

The token is kept until the owner revokes access or deletes it from their computer. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, which makes the token stop working immediately. Uploaded videos stay on the owner's YouTube channel, where they can be edited or deleted from YouTube Studio.

Google and YouTube terms

yt-upload's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses the YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Serviceand the Google Privacy Policy also apply.

Changes to this policy

If yt-upload starts using Google data in a new way, this page is updated first and the date at the top changes.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/ElegantCatterpillar/RelayPad/issues.
